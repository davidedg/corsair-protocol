# `07 15 SS 00` - Set the entry of an on-board profile slot (K95 RGB Platinum)

On the K95 RGB Platinum field `0x15` sets the entry of on-board profile slot `SS` (0 to 2) in the slot table ([`0e 17 04`](../../../../0e/17/04/0e1704.md)): 20 bytes from byte 4, the GUID of the profile (16 bytes, in the byte order of its text form), the revision cookie (u24) and `01`. With 20 zero bytes it clears the slot; [`07 17 0e`](../../../17/0e/07170e.md) then empties it.

```
07 15 00 00 1a a3 48 4e ce ce 4c 3e 87 b4 75 df 2f cd b6 3a b5 04 00 01   slot 0, GUID 1aa3484e-..., cookie 0x04b5 = 1205
07 15 02 00 f2 dd 5a 20 fd 76 4a d4 94 d8 aa a5 f3 db 85 da 08 00 00 01   slot 2, GUID f2dd5a20-..., cookie 8
07 15 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00   clear slot 2
```

Check the status ([`0e 17 0d`](../../../../0e/17/0d/0e170d.md)) after it: after a clear the reply can take up to 2.2 s. The GUID and the cookie are also written in the [PROFILE.I](../../../../../formats/cape/profile-i.md) of the slot. How a slot is written: [devices/k95p.md](../../../../../devices/k95p.md#write-a-slot).
